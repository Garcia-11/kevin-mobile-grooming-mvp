Kevin’s Mobile Grooming — Product Walkthrough
English AI narration generated in Runway. Animated real application captures and fictional practice bookings.

Kevin runs a mobile dog grooming business. This MVP brings his booking requests into one clear workflow.

His problem is simple: requests get lost in text messages. The app gives him one place to review and manage them.

Customers sign in to their own account, then request a visit with their dog’s details, the service and a preferred date.

They can also add an optional dog photo and preview it before sending. The saved image is private to the customer and administrator.

A preferred date is a request, not a guaranteed appointment. Kevin still agrees on the exact time and price with the customer.

Contact information, the service address and care notes are collected together. For this walkthrough, all the booking details are fictional.

The customer gets a receipt and a reference number. The screen clearly explains that the appointment is not confirmed yet.

Kevin receives the new request in his workspace. The unread indicator and request list make it easier to see who needs a reply.

Opening a request brings together the dog, service, customer details and care notes. Kevin can contact the customer before agreeing on the visit.

To confirm, Kevin enters the start time, appointment duration and travel time. Here, a visit starts at ten, lasts one hour and reserves twenty minutes for travel.

That reserves the schedule from ten until eleven twenty. The database blocks any confirmed appointment that overlaps the visit or its travel buffer.

An attempt to confirm another visit at ten thirty is rejected. The request stays pending, so Kevin can choose a free time.

At eleven twenty, the next visit can be confirmed. The previous appointment and its travel buffer have finished, so there is no overlap.

For real bookings, Brevo sends confirmation and cancellation emails to the customer’s verified account. This isolated practice workspace sends no email.

If a confirmed visit is cancelled, its time is released. The request remains in the system, and a real customer receives a cancellation email.

Permanent deletion is a separate action with an explicit warning. Here I keep the request; I do not delete it.

Sites and Cloudflare host the application. Supabase manages accounts and data, Brevo sends booking emails, and the source code is available on GitHub.

This MVP assumes one groomer. Route calculation and closed-browser push alerts are future improvements. I used AI to help build and test the solution.
